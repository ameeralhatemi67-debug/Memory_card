## Memory Card 1.4.1: the first public build

Spaced-repetition flashcards with an endless stack. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no server.

- **A hand of cards for navigation:** Home, Memory Lab, a smart **+**, Shop and Account. The **+** writes a card for the deck you are studying, or deals *New deck* / *New card* when you are anywhere else.
- **90 ready-made decks, 2,507 cards** in the shop, including football, anime, flags, car brands and 13 new picture decks.
- **Pictures on cards:** structured layouts or a free canvas, PNG cut-outs, and "blur until revealed".
- **Deck skins and wax seals:** give a deck a picture behind its bar and stamp seals as you reach milestones.
- **13 home-screen widgets and a Quick Settings tile:** goal, streak, a Quick card you answer in place, activity, forecast, membership card and more.
- **New in 1.4.1:** Settings shows the real version number (it said 1.0.0) and *Send feedback* includes it.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. Widgets look best on Android 12 or newer.
