## Memory Card 1.6.0: scratch, Make 24 and a reveal for every deck

- **Make 24:** 12 Math decks; build 24 from four numbers right on the card.
- **Scratch to reveal:** scratch silver foil off the answer, and feel it buzz.
- **A reveal per deck:** use yours for all decks, the ones you pick, or each deck's own.
- **Stuck on a Make 24?** It shows a way one step at a time, and only a way you have not found before counts when a card comes back.
- **Settings › Study** shows a small moving picture of how each reveal works, and lets you set how hard scratching vibrates.
- **Fix:** undoing a swipe no longer leaves that review in the card's history.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. Widgets look best on Android 12 or newer.
